## Why

The project already has anatomical tools, exact full/cheap HDC descriptions and
case recall. Signed teaching is available in an isolated worker, growth remains
a prototype, and production records identity without using it in condition
reasoning. These joins must work together before claiming a complete AI system.

## What Changes

- Publish all warranted calls with their actual signs, identities and view scopes.
- Preserve signed teaching through anatomically supported, frozen-parent growth.
- Make recalled identity govern clinical evidence eligibility and bounded action.
- Connect independent corrections to contextual classical memory and procedures.
- Verify the same operation through the production entry point and offline envelope.

This declares the existing design's unfinished behavior. It does not prescribe a
universal episode framework, perfect outlines, a new encoder, fitted cutoffs or ML.
The worked example's second round is withdrawn as evidence of growth.
