## Purpose

Connect the existing knee representations and tools into a scoped signed memory,
anatomically supported corpus-growth loop and active classical-AI production system.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Calls retain their exact asserted scope
The system SHALL retain positive, negative and alternative-identity calls with their original source, reviewer, question and view scope. Silence SHALL NOT create a negative. Adaptation SHALL preserve which views were called.

#### Scenario: Rejected sagittal region with unasserted coronal region
- **WHEN** a reject records only sagittal negative views
- **THEN** publication writes a sagittal negative and leaves coronal unasserted.

#### Scenario: Correct mixed-view teaching
- **WHEN** a reviewer confirms femur on coronal and rejects femur on sagittal
- **THEN** both scoped facts and their usable source bundles survive publication.

### Requirement: Recognition and automated admission have different obligations
Identity answering SHALL treat unavailable anatomy as unknown, never negative. Automated new-case admission SHALL require confident joint recognition, affirmative applicable anatomical support for the claimed identity and scope, and no known contradiction. Independently reviewed teaching SHALL NOT require automated anatomy to be complete.

#### Scenario: Unknown-only anatomy
- **WHEN** recognition proposes an identity but no applicable anatomical support is established
- **THEN** the candidate remains an unadmitted proposal, with its recognition evidence retained.

#### Scenario: Supported partial observation
- **WHEN** confident joint recognition has applicable anatomical support and no contradiction, while other views or relations are unavailable
- **THEN** admission is permitted at the warranted scope without requiring every rule or all three planes.

#### Scenario: Plausible location that a negative case opposes
- **WHEN** the anatomical check passes on location and a confirmed negative case of the proposed identity speaks against the candidate
- **THEN** the candidate is not admitted, and its record keeps the pass as location only.

### Requirement: An anatomical pass is a law check
An anatomical pass SHALL be recorded as the relation it checked, which establishes location, never tissue identity. Admission SHALL NOT require any single check to tell an object from its neighbouring tissue alone; that is the joint work of positive and negative recall. Map distances SHALL guide candidate search and SHALL NOT become admission limits without a warranted anatomical meaning drafted from the pictures and reviewed.

#### Scenario: A location pass does not establish tissue
- **WHEN** a candidate passes "femur above the tibia"
- **THEN** its admission evidence names that relation, axis and intervals as location support only.

#### Scenario: A departure from the map's expectation
- **WHEN** a candidate lies away from the map's expected site but breaks no applicable relation to established anatomy
- **THEN** the departure alone neither contradicts nor refuses it.

### Requirement: Anatomical evidence measures the current study
Anatomical checking SHALL use the directions constrained by available native planes and preserve unknown directions. A candidate window SHALL NOT be treated as anatomical occupancy, and rival identity hypotheses SHALL NOT count as two established physical objects.

#### Scenario: Partial plane constrains one direction
- **WHEN** a native plane constrains one relevant anatomical direction but leaves others undetermined
- **THEN** that direction informs the check and the others remain unknown.

#### Scenario: Overlapping coarse windows
- **WHEN** two candidate windows overlap without established tissue occupancy
- **THEN** overlap alone supplies no anatomical contradiction.

### Requirement: Growth preserves all teaching and excludes all source kinds
Each growth round SHALL read one frozen parent. The child SHALL retain positive, negative and alternative cases, paired full/cheap descriptions, calls and lineage. Whole-study exclusion SHALL cover every teaching record kind. Recognition-derived cases SHALL NOT become independent confirmations.

#### Scenario: Study represented only by negative teaching
- **WHEN** that study is evaluated with source exclusion
- **THEN** none of its negative, alternative or positive records contributes to comparisons or opposing envelopes.

#### Scenario: Next generation retains counterevidence
- **WHEN** a child memory receives warranted additions
- **THEN** its next-round reader still hears the parent's negative evidence and identifies each addition's frozen parent and source.

### Requirement: Growth does not widen a recalled claim
Admission SHALL retain only the scope actually warranted by compatible joint evidence. Opposed or unasserted facets SHALL NOT be silently promoted. Missing or malformed scope SHALL return a named unresolved outcome rather than a whole-case assertion.

#### Scenario: Recalled evidence has no usable scope
- **WHEN** a proposed automatic admission cannot establish its asserted views or region
- **THEN** it remains unadmitted with a named reason and does not acquire an unrestricted call.

### Requirement: Identity recall affects the decisions that consume tissue
Condition reasoning and bounded observation requests SHALL consume recalled identity and its scope. Tissue established as the wrong object SHALL NOT silently answer that object's condition question. Unresolved identity SHALL NOT imply clinical absence.

#### Scenario: Proposed femur is recognized as other tissue
- **WHEN** the original proposed region is not warranted as femur
- **THEN** it cannot provide femur-specific decisive evidence, and the named conflict or missing observation reaches the condition consumer.

#### Scenario: Recognized object supports a pending question
- **WHEN** identity is warranted at the relevant scope
- **THEN** its compatible observations remain available to condition recall and targeted follow-up.

### Requirement: Independent correction teaches without inventing a censor
The system SHALL keep the recalled case, expectation, independent correction and decoded differences together. A corrected signed case can teach immediately. Specialized near-miss updates SHALL compare a named significant difference against that K-line's failures and successes, preserving scope and the existing warrant mechanism.

#### Scenario: Several banks express one anatomical difference
- **WHEN** a confirmed failure differs in several readings because of one named anatomical property
- **THEN** raw bank count does not decide whether it is a near miss.

#### Scenario: Difference has not discriminated failures from successes
- **WHEN** a candidate difference has not warranted a censor or split
- **THEN** the correction remains available as signed teaching and no specialized veto is invented.

### Requirement: Classical memory remains active after corpus construction
Recalled contextual knowledge SHALL reach interpretation, condition reasoning or selection of an existing named procedure. Corpus population SHALL NOT be reported as the entire learning process. Task outcomes SHALL retain the teaching source appropriate to the question.

#### Scenario: Corrected action can change later routing
- **WHEN** a task's independently confirmed experience establishes a contextual interpretation and useful procedure
- **THEN** subsequent matching context can reactivate that knowledge in an actual decision or tool request, with the source visible.

### Requirement: A diagnosis carries across the views of its object
A diagnosis clear in one view of an object SHALL make every other view in which the same object's identity is established a positive presentation of that diagnosis, sourced to the view where it was clear. A view whose support is other tissue SHALL carry no condition evidence. The carry SHALL be positive only.

#### Scenario: Diagnosis clear in sagittal, object present coronally
- **WHEN** a condition is established for an object in its sagittal view and the object's identity is established in its coronal view
- **THEN** the coronal view alone recalls that object's case as a positive presentation of the condition.

#### Scenario: A wrongly named facet
- **WHEN** a case's sagittal support was called other tissue
- **THEN** none of that case's condition calls speak through its sagittal banks.

### Requirement: Identity growth does not manufacture clinical truth
Normal and diagnosed memories SHALL use the actual scope of visual, label and report evidence. Identity-only calls SHALL NOT create diagnoses. Whole-condition recall SHALL hear both sides and decide only with warranted condition-relevant tissue and no necessary contradiction.

#### Scenario: Object identity without a clinical call
- **WHEN** recognition adds a femur identity case without a localized clinical finding
- **THEN** it creates no fracture or contusion label for that bone.

#### Scenario: Warranted whole-normal configuration
- **WHEN** recall of an independently diagnosed normal whole-condition case is warranted, compares condition-defining tissue, and has no applicable contradictory finding
- **THEN** it may decide the condition absent without separate negative findings at every possible site (Rohan's decision, 2026-10-22).

#### Scenario: Local normal is not whole-normal recall
- **WHEN** only one site has a normal finding and no warranted whole-normal configuration answers the question
- **THEN** other possible sites remain unresolved and the local finding alone does not rule out the condition.

#### Scenario: Conflicting whole-condition configurations
- **WHEN** warranted normal and diagnosed whole-condition configurations both speak
- **THEN** the condition remains open even if their comparisons used different defining sites; neither nearest-case priority nor averaging resolves the disagreement.

### Requirement: Production remains one bounded offline operation
The existing production entry point SHALL run squint-first with shared preparation, bounded study lifetimes and question-directed finer work. It SHALL write the twelve values in the competition schema using the existing readout, retain required outputs, and verify the complete offline CPU, RAM, storage and time envelope.

#### Scenario: Unsettled question earns bounded work
- **WHEN** cheap recall leaves a question open
- **THEN** only its named relevant region and procedure earn finer work, with no mandatory full-anatomy reconstruction prefix.

### Requirement: Completion is demonstrated by the actual integrated loop
Acceptance SHALL include the real entry point, signed recall, supported admission, a portable child, and a next round consuming it. Comparisons SHALL preserve the mechanism and negative evidence across rounds. Tests or artifact validation alone SHALL NOT establish runtime completion.

#### Scenario: Reproducible two-round evidence
- **WHEN** growth is demonstrated
- **THEN** frozen input identities, scopes, comparison procedure, retained counterevidence and actual new additions are recorded separately from existing teachers.

#### Scenario: First-round admissions inspected before promotion
- **WHEN** the first growth round publishes a child memory
- **THEN** all its admissions are inspected on their pictures before it becomes the next round's parent; later rounds' admissions are spot-checked rather than held, and a wrong admission found either way returns as a scoped corrected negative.
