## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Shared knee readings exchange boundary information generally
The system SHALL consume participating production readings of the same study and
orientation as complementary measurements of the shared knee and its objects.
Its conversation SHALL be derived from the thesis and symbolically traced live
cell implementation. The cell-instance depth-nearness and stacked-cell mechanisms
SHALL NOT become knee identity rules. Behavior SHALL NOT depend on an illustrative
study UID, local territory number, display color or hand-picked sequence pair.

#### Scenario: A boundary is distinct in one weighting
- **WHEN** one reading resolves a boundary that another reading leaves unresolved
- **THEN** the conversation can carry that measured information into the other's native frame and use it to refine the reading, retaining both original measurements and the exchange provenance

#### Scenario: Founder supplies an illustrative coronal pair
- **WHEN** the founder points to blue territory 1 and orange territory 3 as an example of complementary resolution
- **THEN** the implementation remains a general production operation and the example is included among its inspection pictures

### Requirement: Conversation keeps independent evidence and dissent inspectable
The system SHALL preserve original samples, territory proposals, original and
refined traces, and the source of every exchanged constraint. Missing acquisition
SHALL remain missing. Unresolved and disagreeing readings SHALL remain visible.
Coordinate mapping SHALL retain native and patient frames without conflating
equal indices or local region IDs with anatomical identity.

#### Scenario: Another reading cannot measure the proposed boundary
- **WHEN** a transferred constraint encounters missing or ambiguous native support
- **THEN** that outcome remains named and the source reading remains available without a forced ownership transfer

### Requirement: General conversation appears in production and staged photos
The conversation SHALL execute in the production study route using returned
measurement records. Each added operation SHALL emit color before / exchanged
constraints / refined readings image series under data/pictures/object-toolkit,
with source identities and computed receipt/checkpoint standing. Rendering SHALL
observe the returned records and SHALL NOT implement a separate conversation.

#### Scenario: Interrupted conversation run
- **WHEN** a run is interrupted
- **THEN** atomic study checkpoints and source-linked pictures identify what completed, and resumption checks the exact source version before continuing

### Requirement: Native run selection preserves scoped evidence
The system SHALL exchange only original bounded B1 bands, retaining donor and edge
identity, and re-read unchanged recipient pixels with the recipient's own split.
It SHALL select one bounded matching native run despite other nonmatching runs.
Multiple, open, disjoint and missing readings SHALL remain distinct. Derived
intersections SHALL NOT become donors in this pass.

#### Scenario: A donor selects one of several recipient runs
- **WHEN** several native dark runs are observed but only one bounded run intersects the transported source envelope
- **THEN** the record retains all native samples and names that intersection concurrent without averaging the other runs

### Requirement: Sample resolution remains declared
Derived bands SHALL inherit the coarser profile step. Sample-cell envelopes SHALL
include half the declared maximum step beyond sample centres. Their widths SHALL
NOT be interpreted as tissue thickness or calibrated uncertainty.

#### Scenario: Two acquisitions have different sample steps
- **WHEN** a native run intersects a transported sample envelope
- **THEN** the intersection retains the coarser resolution metadata and its actual sample endpoints

### Requirement: Acquired slab coverage remains explicit
The system SHALL preserve signed off-plane distances of every transported band
corner and the plane-normal angle. Raster constraints SHALL require all corners
inside the recipient's acquired SliceThickness slab. Unknown thickness SHALL
remain unknown. This sampling contract SHALL NOT become an object-identity or
depth-nearness rule.

#### Scenario: Transported band has no acquired slab support
- **WHEN** a band corner falls outside the recipient slab or the slab thickness is unknown
- **THEN** the exchange retains outside_slab or slab_support_unknown and cannot cut either map

### Requirement: Two parent-preserving connectivity readings remain explicit
Convex band supercover SHALL include pixel-cell boundary ties. Four-connected
subdivisions SHALL remain inside original parent labels and retain every piece
and explicit barrier cell.
The concurrent map SHALL use only concurrent barriers. A separate conditional
map SHALL also include donor-only barriers. Each peer's masks SHALL remain
inspectable; their OR SHALL describe geometry, never votes or ownership.

#### Scenario: Recipient has available non-dark samples
- **WHEN** an original bounded donor band is covered but the recipient's entire sampled profile has no dark run
- **THEN** the peer_only reading constrains the conditional map while the concurrent map remains unchanged, and both original readings remain available

### Requirement: Repeated numeric work answers to its reference
Native sampling and sample-band rasterization SHALL have strict reference/JIT
twins, with no silent fallback. SAME SHALL compare every serialized B2 array,
named decision, source link and scalar within the receipt environment. Proof
standing SHALL bind the writer, serializer, verifier, all production sources,
receipt and exact artifacts. A stale link SHALL NOT be reported current.

#### Scenario: A saved array or writer changes
- **WHEN** a descriptor, artifact, source version or environment no longer matches the recorded proof
- **THEN** computed standing names the proof unverified or stale
