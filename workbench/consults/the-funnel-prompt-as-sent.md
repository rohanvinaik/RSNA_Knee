# The lead's reading, written before this consult — labelled as the lead's inference

**(a) The pipeline is a funnel, not a row of total stages.** Each lens is cheap, lossy and certain about what it excludes. Its output is a shared fact that every later computation consumes to decide WHERE to look at all. The first lens, the background, removes the air with no false exclusions, and from then on nothing reads the air: not the digits, not the distance transform, not the traces, not the pairings. His arithmetic, which the lead takes as his: the search space halves at the first lens and the uncertainty of every later resolution falls with the product of the exclusions. The build instead made every stage total over the whole field, so each stage is honest in isolation and none consumes another's exclusion; the readings meet only at the end, in the bundles, after the compute has been spent everywhere. That is "functionalities in islands" by another route: islands that run in sequence but do not narrow one another.

**(b) Two resolutions for two jobs.** The squint (the tile grid, the thesis's collapse) is for localisation and targeting: at tile scale the two bones are already resolvable with near-certainty, at a cost two orders of magnitude below native.
