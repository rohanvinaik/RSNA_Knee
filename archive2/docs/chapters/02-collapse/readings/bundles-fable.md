Either the founder said "two members" (then the table is wrong and the word is his), or the ruling as quoted is the lead's paraphrase presented as a ruling. ## 1. Where vocabulary/priors have been substituted

**a. The conclave was applied at the wrong level.** The founder's units of the conclave are *lenses*, *signals*, *analyses*, *traits*: "a conclave of **lenses**", "all the **signals** to produce legible output from their path", "the superposition of all these **analyses**", and his trait list ("bright", "smooth", "straight-ish", "giant", "two of them", "defined border"). The shipped unit seats two *populations feeding the same tercile edge* — two routes to one "bright" reading — and names them with the conclave's vocabulary ("members", "concur/dissent"). Nothing in his quoted words says the conclave is convened between two statistics of the same trait. Whether that is a legitimate extension or the lead's prior wearing his words, I cannot prove from this document; it depends on the answer to §0.

**b. `concur` / `dissent` is a binary-agreement frame; his frame is additive.** His words: "information is *addative*", "superposition", "keep everything, including where things conflict". He never names agreement at all. A per-place label that sorts places into agree/disagree is the lead's taxonomy. Keeping *both digit maps* satisfies "keep everything"; the six-way label is derived product the founder did not ask for. Not an error — he said "I'll resolve what we report" — but it is lead vocabulary, and it is the vocabulary §3 then inherits.

**c. "conjunction" (§3) is the lead's word, and it is the wrong connective under his words.** The lead: "the object being the **conjunction** across them rather than any one." The founder: "defined to exist in specific **patterns/combinations**", and "Just because one lens... isn't able to resolve sufficiently, doesn't mean the rest aren't able to say anything... quorum is a *population*-wide state." A conjunction is exactly the attendance-list reading he rejected: one unwritable row and the object is undefined. "Named absence, never rows of zero" handles how the absence is *stored*; it does not rescue "conjunction" as the *meaning*. This is provable drift from "additive" and "population-wide."

**d. "mechanical", "bundle", "banks", "row counts" — unverifiable from here.** These are attributed to `docs/OBJECT_MODEL.md` as a recording of H2a2/H2b. I cannot check that the figures say "row counts over named banks"; I can only note that the founder's quoted words say "high-dimensional representation across various physical traits," which is compatible with that reading but does not compel it.

## 2. Does §3 follow from his words?

Partly. Follows: "superposition of a high-dimensional representation across various physical traits" → a profile that is a set of per-trait readings. Does **not** follow, from his own trait list:

- "there are **two of them**, roughly split in the middle"
- "takes up most of the image space... **the center object in the whole image**"
- "straight-ish"

These are relations between objects, and between an object and the field — not per-place counts of one object. The thesis statement at the top of this document says the system "constructs relations between them." The lead's sentence claims the `vectors` stage "makes [all seven traits] mechanical," but the structure as described — "the bundle of its places as row counts over named banks," "per object" — can represent "bright" and "defined border," plausibly "smooth," and cannot represent "two of them split in the middle" without a relational bank that is not mentioned. **The claim overreaches the structure.** Whether the thesis figures contain relational banks is a question, not something I can assert.

## 3. The count table as "visual output"

His words, 2026-10-11: "a visual output that shows the actual reasoning process **on images themselves** to demonstrate visual reasoning"; "**photo output at every state**." His words, 2026-10-12: "it's just a printed copy of the analysis that is already required."

A count table per object satisfies the second sentence and fails the first. The places being counted have positions on the image; a table discards them. Both sentences are his; the lead has acted on the cheaper one and dropped "on images themselves" and "photo." That is a narrowing. (Reporting the gap, not proposing what the picture should be.)

Note also: the **shipped** unit's description mentions no picture of the two digit maps or the dissent places. "Photo output at every state" — was one emitted for this state?

## 4. Is `vectors` the next step under his words?

His words on sequencing are: "the orchestration of the thesis stages... **as the thesis itself lays out**", "everything registered and consuming/consumed by the main production pipeline", and "a single study... single entrypoint... compute everything... single end-to-end production pipeline." He did not name a next stage; he named a property of the whole. So:

- If the thesis lays out `vectors` as the stage that consumes the lens stage's output, building it next is his. If the lead chose it because it is the stage that most resembles the lead's own "a bone is bright and smooth..." reading, that is the prior he warned about. I cannot tell which from this document. **Q:** what does the thesis say comes next?
- "There is no such thing as a 'small piece' of a single object. All of it" bears directly: `vectors` is "currently unbuilt and **not called by the route at all**." His failure mode is "functionalities in islands... there isn't an actual working pipeline." Building `vectors` as a unit that is not wired into the route in the same unit reproduces, verbatim, the condition he described. Whatever the next stage is, his words make "registered and consumed by the route, with photo output" part of the definition of having built it.
