# The two processes as exact rules: the lead's reading, written before the Codex check (2026-10-14)

The rules are `openspec/changes/the-funnel/design.md` §9. This file is the lead's own reading of
them, written before Codex's answer is opened (the consultation protocol), so the two can be
compared: agreement is evidence, disagreement is a question for him.

## What the rules say, as a child would say it

Knee pictures always look the same: a big bone on top, a big bone underneath, a dark line between
them across the middle. Squint at every picture (cheap); throw away the black; look down the middle
of the leg: the line is the darkest spot with bright above and below. Everything joined to the top
bright part is the top half, to the bottom bright part the bottom half. The pictures where the line
sits in the middle cut through the joint; pick the middle one, and on the side-to-side pictures the
middle of each side too. Box the line and look closely only inside the box: each thing is found
from the spot deepest inside it; the biggest above the line is the top bone, below it the bottom
bone, then the kneecap in front of the top bone just above the line, and the small bone tucked under
one end of the bottom bone, which is the outer side. Everything else is found by where it sits next
to those. Then ask what is wrong with each one, the way an eye would.

## What was measured before the rules were written

On the one study, every slice of every acquisition was looked at (contact sheets), and the cheap
halves were run on every slice. The existing seeds (the top and bottom fifths of the leg) found the
line on the joint slices of the coronal and on most of the sagittal, and missed the sagittal's inner
compartment: the upper half flooded down the bright front of the leg and took the lower bone.
Seeded instead from the bright stretches either side of the deepest enclosed dip, the line sits at
the middle of the picture on every joint slice of both fat-bright views and the crossing follows the
joint. On the fat-dark the old reading wrapped round the bright fluid at the sides; the fat-dark is
the same picture as the fat-bright, so the halves are carried across rather than read twice.

## Where the lead thinks the rules are weakest (written before reading Codex)

1. **The top bone can show as two round ends.** On the coronal back still the upper bone's two
   rounded ends are separate in the picture, and inside a box that cuts the bone 40 mm above the
   line they may be two territories. "The territory with the highest peak in the upper half" would
   then take one end and miss the other, and the band would read on one side only. The child's
   version already says it: on each side, the top bone's round end sits on the bottom bone's flat
   top. The fix the lead expects: the bones are the big territories facing the line (each whose
   peak is in its half and whose pixels reach the line), so either end counts, and the band is read
   per side between what faces across it.
2. **The small bone versus a lump of fat.** On both acquisitions a lump of fat reads like the inside
   of a bone (bright where marrow is bright, dark where marrow is dark), so "a territory under one
   end of the bottom bone" could take fat. What tells them apart by eye is that the small bone is
   round, ringed by a dark rim, and tucked against the bottom bone's outer corner. The rule should
   say that, and the two views' answers stay side by side.
3. **The stills at fixed fractions of the run.** One fifth, one half and four fifths of the run are
   the middle of each side by the fixed layout. Off by a slice or two they still sit in the right
   part; a buffer of slices is his to widen. The thesis would pick each part's sharpest slice; the
   fixed fraction is the simpler sentence.
4. **The crop's edge.** The distance from dark treats the box's edge as an obstacle, so the bones'
   peaks are pulled toward the line, and edges along the box are `frame_clipped`. Neither must be
   read as anatomy; the rules say so.
5. **The twelve questions.** Each is what an eye asks of its element. How much counts (how thin is
   thin, how bright is lit up) is his, derived experimentally; the rules build the measurement and
   the picture, and every row stays `form_not_given`.

## What the lead is not sure of

Whether the axial needs the same treatment first or can follow (the rules defer it: the paired views
carry every one of the twelve). Whether the pocket above the kneecap fits in a box nine tiles above
the line on every knee.
