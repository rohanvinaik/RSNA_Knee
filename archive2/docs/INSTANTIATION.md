## What every stage owes

Rendering, performance engineering and clean modular design are equal requirements.
[STAGE_CONTRACT.md](STAGE_CONTRACT.md) defines them. `just unit` prints declared route metadata,
whole next-open tasks and structural place diagnostics. `just places` does not establish runtime
reachability for every branch, and renderer modules do not prove successful drawings.
Fresh executions and gallery/ledger records supply the complementary evidence.

## Open meaning and implementation obligations

The founder still supplies local structural normalisation, expected correspondence `e`, numeric
abstention projection after inspection, and domain meaning for the next atomic correspondences.
Valley/tercile ambiguity is settled for the current static-middle contract: own-population tercile
edges; no tissue witness and no spread band. Multi-acquisition support and dispatch remain separate.

One compute-contract idiom and archival of the five shelf packages are already settled, not pending
choices. The anatomy inventory is design work, not a demand to label runtime records. Current
certification and wiring gaps are owned by the active OpenSpec checklists and the dated
[reconciliation evidence](chapters/02-collapse/measurements/documentation-reconciliation-20261012.md).

The laterality design keeps secondary witnesses for concurrence and never silently promotes them
to independent authority. The exact fallback when the primary witness is absent remains a founder
question where older passages differ. No reconciliation changes that policy.

## Work ownership and evidence

The immediate change is `every-stage-renders`; the full architecture is `knee-object-model`;
physical/conversation/contour contracts belong to `object-resolution-toolkit`. See
[BUILD_PLAN.md](BUILD_PLAN.md) and [the OpenSpec index](../openspec/README.md).

A miner returns facts and locations after whole-unit reads. The lead owns interpretation,
contracts, intent tests, bars, wiring and admission. Bodies, renderers and serializers may be
mechanically delegated only under the founder's recorded authorization and after lead review.
Second-reader critique supplies evidence/questions, never design authority.
