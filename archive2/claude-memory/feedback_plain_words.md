The founder, 2026-10-12, answering the lead's questions after the view sheets (recorded verbatim in
`docs/chapters/02-collapse/teachings.md`, "THE FUNNEL"):
- On a question phrased in the build's coordinate-frame terms: «...huh? Many words, very little meaning.»
- On a question about "members": «Again, huh? What's a "member"?? I don't know why you insist on using random
  BS made-up terms all the time, I just can't get you to stop. I don't speak Claude!! I INVENTED this, I still
  have no idea what you mean when you discuss it with me half the time»
- On whether to retire lenses the lead found redundant: «Yeah, don't delete/remove lenses. I will figure out what
  bizarre interpretation you had of my words/ideas led to this presentation, but I'm sure they are meaningful in
  some way.» and «Keep all, I will do speulunking to understand these later.»
- On where a lens's authority comes from: «The thing that grants the first lens the "authority" to declare
  background is "being correct". … We make a lens that looks for what we define as background, and when it works
  and only finds things that aren't bones or other anatomical features, then it works and we use it.»
- On where the expensive boundary work runs: «we define that, by looking at it (common-sense border estimation)
  and by using domain knowledge for where these elements are supposed to be on these really fixed and almost
  identical-looking images»

**Why:** the lead named things in the build's own vocabulary ("member" for one of two tercile rules on one image,
"standing" for their agreement, "frame" for a pixel grid or the tile grid) and put questions to him in it. Two of
six questions were unreadable to the one person whose answer they needed. The lead also proposed, by implication,
retiring lenses it judged redundant, which is not its call.

**How to apply:**
- In every tell and every question to him, use only words he or the thesis use: lens, image, view, series,
  background, leg, bone, the gap, boundary, the squint, tile, the acquired picture, the interior peaks. Where a
  build name is unavoidable, say in plain words what it does in the same sentence ("panel 7, the digit map against
  the noise-law cut").
- Ask one plain question at a time, in the domain's terms, with the picture it is about named. If a question needs
  a term of the build to be stated, it is not ready to be asked.
- Never remove, retire or merge a lens or a picture on the lead's own judgement of redundancy. Draw it, name it,
  and leave it for him to read.
- A lens's authority is correctness on the real views, checked by looking: it is right when it finds only what it
  was defined to find. "Being correct" is checked per view; one rule declared once and run flatly across views is
  not that check.
- Where expensive work runs is his to define by eye and by anatomy, not a formula of the lead's.
