The founder, 2026-10-10, after the loop had been used through two chapters and he wanted it kept:

- «Make sure you're using cheap agents/advisors to look into the thesis and the cell tracking project build as
  needed when you're uncertain. Both are oracles of truth--one on theory and explanation, one on literal
  engineering. One can be read, one symbolically traced, but the workflow you should be using is to work it out
  with fable/codex and ground on truth with cheap agents mining on the text/advisors on questions you need to
  solve. I can guide where needed, but this is a nice, clean workflow loop you've been using, I'd like to persist
  it so we can continue this way.»

**Why it exists.** Before it, the lead read everything itself and filled a context window without delivering a
tell (2026-10-10, the compaction that cost a whole session), or proposed builds on priors without looking at the
substrate at all. The loop fixes both: the reading happens, but in an agent's context, and the lead keeps its own
for the synthesis.

**How to apply.**

- **Two oracles, each with its own way in.** The thesis settles theory and explanation and is **read** — whole
  sections, by a mining agent where the section is long. The cell-tracking build settles literal engineering and
  is **symbolically traced** — Serena `find_symbol` and `find_referencing_symbols`, then the located unit read
  whole. Neither is opinion; both are truth.
- **A mining agent may now be launched without asking.** This is a standing authorization that amends the earlier
  rule (project `CLAUDE.md`) that one was launched only when he asked each time. A miner reads named units whole
  and returns **facts with their locations and verbatim quotations**, states plainly what it did not look at, and
  offers **no judgement**. Information flows downward only: it informs the lead of facts, never of meaning,
  intent, or what to build, and nothing it says is a premise until checked against the founder's words.
- **Fable and Codex are second readers, never warrant** (law 9). Write the lead's own reading to the chapter's
  `readings/` **before** opening either answer. They may report a provable error with its proof, or ask a
  question. Independent agreement is evidence — **but only where each reader reasoned from its own reading**,
  since agreement cannot validate a mistaken premise supplied by a brief both were handed (observed
  2026-10-11). Disagreement is a question for the founder. They have repeatedly caught real errors — a trap
  written into the code in the same round, a quoted constant that was wrong, an overclaim about what a
  mechanism disposes of, and a convexity proof that was about a different update than the one the code performs.
- **"Fable" means the Anthropic API path**, and nothing else:
  `~/Projects/SquishOS/ops/agents/bin/anthropic-consult --prompt <file> [--image <png>] --out <reading.md>`
  (details in `anthropic-consultation.md`). A sub-agent given `model: "fable"` is **not** this — it runs inside
  the session, bills the session, cannot be shown a picture, and its reading is not independent of the brief
  this session wrote. Recorded 2026-10-11 after the lead made exactly that substitution and the founder had to
  say: «I LITERALLY set it up to use some of my available API credits. That was the POINT». Every call leaves a
  `-metadata.json` beside its answer; read the directory before re-running one, because a re-run spends credits
  on an answer already in hand.
- **The advisor is for the question itself**: before a multi-file plan locks, when the same failure returns
  twice, and before declaring work done.
- **Measure on the real corpus, through Chapter 1's entry point.** A stage that has only seen synthetic data is
  not built. Running stage 1 over the 58 showed 0 of 336 series concurring and found a truncation fault the unit
  tests could not see, because the synthetic fixtures lacked the property that breaks it.
- **Record to the chapter's own folder before the next step** — a reading to
  `docs/chapters/NN-<package>/readings/`, a measurement to `measurements/`, the chapter's `README.md` kept
  current — so a compaction costs one unit rather than a session.
- **Then tell him**: the mechanism as read, what was measured, and where the lead is still uncertain. He guides
  from there.

**What never delegates:** the synthesis, the interpretation, the telling, and every judgement.

**The founder is not the end of the loop; he is the one who corrects a misframing.** His words, 2026-10-10: «I am
the one that guides misunderstandings, like your "pleural effusion brightniess" issue that you were stuck on.»
Nothing else in the loop can do that job: a miner returns what the text says but cannot know the lead asked the
wrong question; Fable and Codex reason *inside* the framing they are handed; the advisor reads the same
transcript and inherits the same blind spot.

**The signal that it is his:** an apparent **blocker**. «if you simply trust that this IS a genuine
implementation of computer vision, then problems like that CAN'T be definitive blockers or unresolvable
incongruities. The answer *exists*. We simply have to work it out together.» So when the lead's conclusion is
that something cannot work, that is evidence of a missing understanding, never a finding. It goes to him at once,
with the framing laid bare and **no verdict attached**: "here is where I am stuck and here is how I am framing
it", never "here is why this is impossible". The loop re-enters at the grounding step once he has redirected it.

The worked case: the lead reported that a level map was impossible, because on a fluid-sensitive sequence the
brightest tissue is fluid. He replied that confluent versus sparse plates have the same global scale-up, which
named where to look and said the answer already existed. The loop resolved it in one pass afterwards, and could
not have found it alone.

The loop is written into `docs/SPINE.md` under "The working loop", which is loaded every session.
