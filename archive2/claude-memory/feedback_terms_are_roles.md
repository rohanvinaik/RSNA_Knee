The founder, 2026-10-10, after the compaction, answering the lead's Chapter 2 tell (recorded verbatim in
`src/knee/collapse/__init__.py`):
- «"Black" isn't a color. It's the structural elements of the problem space that can be resolved out, because they
  never contain information or data that is itself informative for positive resolution of the categorization we
  care about … That isn't the same as "junk", it's important to have positive assignment of what that space is …
  "black" is the space of the data we are given that does not actually ever relate in any way to the 12 possible
  categories, and the context that is needed to resolve them.»
- «knee images have a GINORMOUS normalization flag--the giant fucking black-ish line that is the connection point
  between the tibia and femur … Knee imaging is HIGHLY structured … It's about discovering structure and noting
  where we actually can have "surprise" vs where the image is simply uninteresting or common across knees»
- «"Speck" refers to appropriate characterization of scale.»
- «A lens is a single measurement that is a lossy representation of the problem space that allows reconstruction of
  the problem as a decomposed structure … I don't know what "focus" has to do with this … This sounds like LLM
  slop, where you read a word and simply hallucinated the entire concept it's meant to encode»
- «Every element of the knee as an anatomical object has a "normal" representation as a superposition of all 58
  movies. The "normal" knee is the "normal" parts of each of the 58 movies, which are all the parts not labeled or
  impacted by the category of issue they are labeled as having.»
- «Synovitis is...Synovitis? Is that not a categoy of label? Why are you trying to reject the competition labels??»

**Why:** the lead's tell carried the cell instance's specifics as if they were the concepts. It asked which object
"the lens" is and whether the four focus measures of §4.2 carry to MRI. It read "black" as dark pixels (a noise-floor
filter) and "speck" as a width cutoff. And it asked what the Synovitis label "should be read as".

**How to apply:**
- Read each thesis term for its role in the decomposition, never for its content in the cell instance. The cell
  build's four lenses measured focus because that fact decomposed the microscope's problem. A knee lens is whatever
  single lossy measurement decomposes the knee's problem.
- Black is named, never discarded and never an intensity: the data that never bears on any of the 12 or on the
  context that resolves them. Naming it sharpens what remains. In the knee it is anchored on the knee's structure
  (the joint line) and on what medicine already normalizes.
- A speck is a matter of scale and reach: below the smallest resolvable, diagnosable damage, or beyond the reach a
  finding can have from the joint. Those sizes and reaches are established from anatomy and the data, never picked.
- The competition's 12 labels stand. Find each category's profile in the data; never ask whether a category is
  meaningful or how it should be read.
- The platonic knee is not one image: at each anatomical element it is the superposition of the 58 studies' normal
  parts there, each study contributing all but the parts labeled or impacted by its positive categories.
