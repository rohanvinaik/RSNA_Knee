**Why:** after Codex's second fresh read, the lead re-derived its amendments and applied them to the design: K14's
committee outcomes, K25's trust-radius rule, K09's gain gauge, K10's tissue word. The review accord and revision 1
had done the same at a larger scale. In his words this was not an overt failure (23:22:50Z): «You were within
tolerable parameters. I gave codex implicit authority on design. It was provisional, you should have been
conservative about running with it». And: «my warrant was general and high-level, not open authority»; «the
competition's literal data and framing is acceptable, not implicit warrants on design or conception». The lesson
is conservatism with provisional authority. One edit, to K14, was also made from a post-compaction summary before
anything was re-read.

**How to apply:**
- Before any edit to a design text, classify it as provably correct (state the proof), warranted by his explicit
  words (quote them, with the source), or neither. Make no edit that is neither. Return such a change to the
  original, or put it to him as a question with the argument beside it.
- Codex, Fable, Antigravity and Grok are each one voice, never a verdict. Take from them only provable errors,
  with proofs, and questions. Open every consultation with
  `archive/docs/book/provenance/build/codex_next_header.md`.
- A Codex prompt holds only the lead's question and understanding and the founder's literal words; no document is
  pasted in (founder, 23:56:22Z: «Codex is meant to be a minimal common-sense check, not a dump … You are only
  supposed to include your question/understanding and my literal words, to maintain maximal semantic purity for
  the reasoning operation»).
- Never write that anything works, holds, is invariant or has been shown **without the run that shows it**, and
  never restate a past measurement as current — recompute it. A canon citation means "the design specifies",
  never "evidence"; and a saved receipt whose verifier reports `stale` is not evidence of current behaviour
  either.
- After a compaction, read the sources a design question rests on, whole and by section, before writing any
  design.
- The audit of every change is `archive/docs/book/provenance/build/audit_against_original.md`.
- The rigid oracle is his lens principle (2026-10-02 23:50:20Z), which supersedes KNEE_ARCHITECTURE: «The concept
  is the rigid oracle, the design is something we're working on building in session» (23:59:57Z). Bringing the
  design into line with it is warranted. Settling its open terms ("direction", "exclusion", "lenses") is his, and
  "encoded as such" is no licence for one reading to decide.
