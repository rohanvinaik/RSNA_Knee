The founder, 2026-10-10, after the lead restated his corrections on black, specks and lenses and proposed to start
building the joint-line flag:
- «Man, you are REALLY eager to build for an agent that just fundamentally misunderstood a core, load-bearing
  element of the project … Did you read the thesis sections on this chapter of the build? Did you look at the literal
  way this is built in the cell tracking project? … Do you know hoe chapter 1 was built? Have you traced any of the
  code for any of this? Do you know the format for encoding the data and/or what it means to derive an assessment
  from the available data?»
- «You came in telling me you knew EXACTLY what to build. But...you didn't look at ANYTHING … Why would you act like
  you knew what we were doing when you didn't read a single fucking thing??????»

**Why:** the lead had read §1, §4 and §5. It had not read what shows how the terms are built: the cassette code, his
words for it, §2, §3, §6, §7, or Chapter 1's code. It filled the gaps from its priors, restated his corrections
fluently, and proposed a build. In the cell build he had already ruled that black is not the camera's dark level
(`cassette/toolkit.py`): the same error.

**How to apply:**
- Know the thesis: it is the oracle's prose.
- For code (the cell build, this repo), keep a symbolic map: Serena's `find_symbol` and
  `find_referencing_symbols`, or `aider-map` on a git root. When a question or a task touches a
  mechanism, locate the unit that implements it and read that unit whole.
- Never bulk-load documents or packages to satisfy the words of a request. The founder, 2026-10-10,
  when the lead began reading the design documents end to end: «I was talking about a symbolic map of
  the code, actually looking at the substrate when I ask you to do something. Are you LITERALLY planning
  on mass-dumping shit into your context window to satisfy the literal words i'm saying? That is the
  OPPOSITE of what I told you to do.»
- Say what was looked at and what was not. A fluent restatement of his words is not understanding.
- After a correction, tell what is understood and what is not, and propose no build step until he says
  so.
