# The six contracts, in plain words (the lead's; the builders fill their bodies now)

1. **The dome at the squint**, per chosen slice. An OBSTACLE tile is one the first lens cleared (background) OR left undecided (the one-tile buffer at the skin) OR, on a paired plane, one whose two acquisitions read `below_below` OR, with no pair (the axial), one whose median lies at or below the series' split. The INTERIOR is every other kept tile. Height = Euclidean distance in mm from an interior tile to the nearest obstacle tile or the picture's frame. Peaks = local maxima of height standing at least one tile (8 mm) above their surroundings, by the native dome's own prominence rule. Territories = watershed of the height from the peaks within the interior, unseeded tiles 0. Nothing cut on strength; every obstacle, height, peak and label kept.

2. **The selection**, per chosen slice, by the view kind the series table tells. Facts per territory, from the tile centres in the scanner's LPS millimetres (+x patient left, +y posterior, +z superior): tile count, centre, the centre's superior, anterior and left-right coordinates, the superior extent, the most anterior tile centre, whether it touches a cleared or undecided tile, whether it is ringed (every outside neighbour an obstacle). Coronal and sagittal: the two largest territories with at least 4 tiles are the big pair, the more superior named `superior_big`, the other `inferior_big`; a tie in count is broken by superior position and the word "tie" is kept. Sagittal: a territory smaller than `superior_big`, with its centre anterior of `superior_big`'s centre, and its superior coordinate strictly between `inferior_big`'s superior face and `superior_big`'s centre, is `anterior_small` (the largest such). Coronal: a territory smaller than `inferior_big`, ringed, with its centre below `inferior_big`'s superior face and a nonzero left-right offset from it, is `lateral_small_ringed` (the largest such); the sign of that offset gives the laterality sign (+x = "left"), abstained with no witness. Axial: the largest territory is `central_big`; the largest other with its centre anterior of it is `anterior_small`. Everything else `unselected`, kept.

3. **The strip**, per selected object. The native edge is read ONLY at the midpoints of the object's tile boundary segments (where a tile of it meets a tile not of it), each with an outward normal: a profile across the boundary at native resolution over plus and minus 6 mm, read for its dark band by the existing rule, the band's extent in mm, the partner's bands at the same points where a pair exists, and the profile samples as digits against the terciles of the object's own pixels. Nothing else is read at native resolution. The outline (the thesis's three pulls) is deferred to its own contract.

4. **The facing pairs**, per chosen slice: for each pair of selected objects, the direct tile crossings between them and the gap tiles (obstacle tiles touching both); facing when at least one gap tile touches both; touching with no gap is named so; an unselected territory is never in a pair; the least height along the gap tiles is kept as the saddle.

5. **The interstice**, per facing pair. Extent = the gap tiles plus every tile that lies between the two objects' nearest faces along the line joining their centres and within the overlap of their widths across it. Each wall = the strip profiles of that object whose normals point into the extent, each profile's digit sequence from the dark band outward read as plate-then-layer-then-fill, plate-then-fill, or unreadable. Fill = the read pair (of the nine two-acquisition words) holding the most tiles of the extent; a tie is "mixed"; none is "unread". Contents = the four-connected bodies of `below_below` TILES inside the extent, each a round-2 object with position "central" when its centre lies within the inner half of the pair's width across the axis, else "peripheral", and a side from its left-right offset and the sign.

6. **Spans and envelope**, per chosen slice. A span = a four-connected body of `below_below` tiles with no tile inside any interstice that touches both `superior_big` and `inferior_big`; touching one or none, or lying inside an interstice, is named so. The envelope = the four-connected component of the fat-dark series' own `above` tiles touching any interstice's extent; its pouch = its tiles posterior of both big objects' most posterior tile centres; its neck = the narrowest tile row between the pouch and the rest.

7. **The slice choice, already built, from the cell build's trace.** Each of four cheap readings per slice (kept share, dark share, Otsu's split quality over the kept medians, fat share) stands its lens at the slice where it peaks; an exact tie takes the middle of the tied slices; a lens needs eight slices with a reading to stand. The cell build takes the earliest tied slice per plane, keeps every tie per object, and has no such floor.
