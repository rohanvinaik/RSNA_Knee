`just plan-status`, `just plan-next` and `just plan-check` default to `every-stage-renders`.
Name another change explicitly. `just unit` prints the complete first unchecked task unit
from each checklist; it does not decide priority from alphabetical order.

Artifact completion means documents exist. Implementation progress comes from tasks;
checkboxes require their declared evidence. A connected/rendered candidate can remain unchecked
because admission or part of the acceptance contract is still owed. Keep dated execution evidence
separate from historical certificate claims and rerun source/proof checks after changes.

Anatomical names are human design vocabulary; production subjects are indexed structural records.
One-study real-entry-point demonstration and 58-study engineering are distinct obligations.
The later founder rulings recorded in Chapter 2/3 govern earlier proposals where they differ.
