# Proposal

## Why

The project had no agreed structure from resolved geometry to the twelve scored findings. Figure 8's
chapter table was transcribed before the knee's `<object>` was known, and it ordered the build by the
cell project's stages rather than by the knee's own anatomy.

The founder settled that structure on 2026-10-11: a finding is a **predicate violation on an object,
or on a relation between objects**, never a pattern found in a field. The move that makes it work is
that an interstice is **constructed, not detected** — "Interstitial space...is the space between the
two bones we resolve as objects! That's LITERALLY what the term means, and here it's *inherent* in
the structure of the way we build the computer vision project itself!"

This change records that structure as the project's own, so the remaining build is bounded and
ordered by it. The full statement is `docs/OBJECT_MODEL.md`.

It also unblocks `object-resolution-toolkit` tasks 4.1 and 5.1, both of which were explicitly gated on
the founder recording the knee teaching. That teaching is now recorded.

## What Changes

- Declare the four-tier object model as the project's structure: objects (bones first), relations
  (interstices per facing pair), spans (attached to two bones, outside the interstice), and the
  envelope (the capsule). It supersedes the chapter table of `docs/SPINE.md`; the way of working, the
  oracles and every project law are unchanged.
- Complete the object parse by residual pull-out, until every element significant to the knee's
  operation is resolved. Founder: "the roles that are still unassigned in the visual field are a lot
  easier to figure out, since they're definitionally NOT the things you already DO know how to
  resolve."
- Construct interstices as relations between facing objects, each carrying gap, walls, fill and
  contents. Compartments are enumerated by the bones, never searched for.
- Settle medial/lateral from the fibula's geometry rather than the laterality tag, which is absent
  from 181 of 336 series in the historical measurement. Keep secondary witnesses for
  concurrence; abstain when no warranted sign is available. Their independent fallback
  authority remains unresolved and is not supplied by this reconciliation.
- State each of the twelve findings as a predicate on an indexed structural subject (anatomy remains human design vocabulary), and emit twelve confidences.
- Keep recognition mechanical. The thesis's memory, buckets and recall (§8) are not part of the object
  parse; they arrive at the diagnosis stage, for presentations a predicate cannot settle alone.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `knee-objects`: Completes the object parse — every element significant to the knee's operation
  resolved from geometry, with the residual pulled out and nothing significant left unassigned.
- `object-relations`: Defines interstices as constructed relations between facing objects, their
  enumeration by the bones, and their gap, walls, fill and contents.
- `finding-predicates`: Defines each of the twelve findings as a predicate on an indexed object,
  relation, span or envelope, and the emission of twelve confidences.

### Modified Capabilities

None. `physical-measurements`, `geometric-interiors` and `same-knee-conversation` are this change's
substrate and keep their contracts.

## Impact

The change affects the project's documented structure (`docs/OBJECT_MODEL.md` becomes the first read),
the stage packages under `src/knee/`, and the route's table of contents. It does not change Packet A,
B1 or B2 behaviour. No competition data is redistributed; derived records stay under ignored `data/`.

Predicate thresholds are not part of this change. Every bar is written down before the run that judges
it, and no level is chosen to separate gold (project law 4).
