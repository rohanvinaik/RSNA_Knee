## Grounding and limits

Thesis §§5.3–5.5, 6.1–6.2 and 13.5/13.7 were read as complete units.
§5.4 keeps a cleaner set of each reading's own bodies, never a merged picture.
The live cassette `lenses_talk.conversation` calls `smoothed` on each original
picture, then `counted_once`; `smoothed` preserves original membership and records
shed places. `outlines.pulled_shape` bounds movement around each traced point.
The cell's oval, size, flicker curve, depth-nearness and stacked-cell parting are
instance content and are not imported into the knee.

The founder's same-knee relation supplies the reason to exchange measurements.
His coronal example is an inspection case, not a pair, label-ID or color rule.
Coordinate projection expresses a shared object in native arrays; it never
establishes anatomical identity.

## Proposed bounded operation

1. Wait for all original series measurements in a study. Every ordered pair of
   distinct same-orientation readings participates, without sequence selection
   by intensity, region size, gold labels or illustration identity.
2. Carry each donor's bracketed native dark intervals (including original edge
   index and owner) into the recipient patient frame. Keep projected endpoints,
   normal, signed off-plane distance and geometric precision standing. Frames
   that cannot express the same selected plane retain an explicit projection
   outcome; this is a sampling-interface limit, never a test of knee identity.
3. Re-read the recipient's unchanged native signal along that projected normal
   with the existing reference/JIT sampler. Retain complete, missing, clipped,
   open and multiple-interval outcomes. Where both dark intervals overlap, the
   intersection is a concurrent band; its midpoint is the refined trace. Where
   a recipient has no dark interval, keep the donor-only boundary constraint
   separately. Disjoint observed intervals are disagreement, never averaged.
4. Keep the original territory map. As a separate geometric reading, expose
   connected subdivisions within each original territory under the transported
   boundary constraints. A subdivision is conditional on its listed donors; it
   is not an anatomical ownership transfer. Boundary places and every source
   value remain available. Missing or geometrically unrepresented constraints
   do not cut the recipient. Preserve all small subdivisions and gaps.
5. Keep all peer exchanges independent. No iterative exchange of already-refined
   evidence creates circular confirmation. No count of opposing copies of one
   boundary is an additional independent measurement.

## Composition and compute

`run_study` decodes each series once, computes per-series measurements once, then
calls one study conversation over them. Its returned record feeds the existing
writer, Hamilton observer graph, saved-record renderer and verification path.
Rendering performs no resolution. Photos show before, exchanged constraints,
recipient re-read/concurrence, and conditional subdivisions across all views.

NumPy expresses frame maps; SciPy labels connected subdivisions; the already
admitted strict Numba sampler executes repeated native reads. Rasterizing local
boundary segments gets a plain reference first and an exact JIT twin only if
its repeated-loop shape warrants it. Scratch is O(N + P*S) per ordered pair,
not an N-by-P distance matrix. Donors are consumed sequentially. All returned
arrays are newly owned/read-only. Named decisions are pinned separately.

The geometric plane precision and raster traversal must be settled explicitly
before implementation. This reading precedes the independent Fable workshop;
that workshop may expose a provable inconsistency or a question, never authorize
anatomical policy or replace the founder's design.
