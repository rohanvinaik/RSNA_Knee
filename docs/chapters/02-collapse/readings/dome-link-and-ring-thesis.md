## What that forbids, and what it offers

**It forbids using the dome link as a force on a contour.** Two facts do it, and both are the thesis's
own: the bank is written at *just under half* a body's places, and **along the body's upper edge the
links point outward, at foreign centres**. The border is exactly where a pull would act, and it is
exactly where the link is sparsest and most likely to point away from the object. A pull built on it
would be strongest in the wrong direction at the one place it matters.

This is consistent with the earlier reader's finding (`central-pull-fable.md`): *"the watershed is the
central assignment, not a central pull … Calling the watershed 'the central pull' conflates partition
with outline."* The dome link is the *assignment*, written down. It is not the thing that moves points.

**It offers something better, which is measured rather than assumed.** §7's own discriminator:

> L236-237: "Of the places that write a dome link, most of the noise's point **north-east, to a bright
> spot beyond it**. The cell's point **every way, most of them to a center inside it**."

The miner notes correctly that §7 describes these patterns and never says what they *mean*. The
lead's inference, labelled as the lead's: this is a **support test for an arc**, and the right use of
the bank for task 3.8. An arc whose places' links point every way to a centre *inside* is bounding
this object. An arc whose links point one way at a centre *beyond* is bounding something else, and
joining across it would be connecting two objects. That is a reading, not an appearance, so it is the
kind of thing the contract may license a connection on.

## The incongruity to put to the founder

His words at 18:45 describe the **coastline move**: *"minimize length/surface area of the drawn line
while keeping the volume within as close to the pre-smoothed version as possible, with a preference to
pull towards a center mass of the 'object'."* That is §6.1's machinery — shortening at fixed area,
with the oval round two centres **computed from the loop's own area and second moments**.

The figures he pointed at, 21 C/D and 25 D, are the **dome link** — a per-place reading of the
composite, sparse, and outward-pointing at the edge.

These are two different mechanisms, and the one his sentence describes is not the one his figures
show. Which he means decides what 3.8 implements, so it goes to him rather than being chosen here.
