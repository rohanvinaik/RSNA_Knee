## Construction rule for every remaining family

Use the physical anatomy already known to reduce the question before paying for its answer. Cheap background/finite readings, squint extrema, rough leg bounds, known neighbours and the common physical map determine useful slice and spatial regions. Native analysis refines these regions. It does not read the whole frame and merely mask afterward. A bound is a useful prior, not a proof of exact exclusion; a source point is not necessarily an object's centre. Measurements remain objective within their named scope; local boundaries can change distances and regions. Inspect the resulting anatomical description, retain its scope and uncertainty, and continue. Neither perfect bounds nor whole-frame-equivalent output is required for intentional targeting.

Share values with identical real inputs and mathematical meaning, not merely similar-looking functions. Keys must include acquisition, native slice, scope and parameters that change the reading. Distinguish a shared primitive from duplicated adapters; verify runtime identity rather than inferring it from call counts. A cached value is not automatically a useful spatially targeted value. A saved value is not a downstream read. Each new description enters the one map before dependent questions are asked.

Equivalent implementation changes (JIT twins, extraction, cache sharing) preserve answers, including dtype, shapes, categorical meanings and availability. Scope changes are evaluated and recorded as scope changes. Keep all computed information under the existing retention rule. No new default-off diagnostics or dropped arrays are authorized by this audit. Optimize persistence without losing information, or bring a concrete retention choice to Rohan. No speed claim without appropriate paired measurement; pixels visited and cache handoffs are separate facts.

## Why efficiency and correctness arise together

Rohan explicitly reaffirmed that efficiency and correctness are inherently connected and flow from the information-theoretic computer vision thesis. His wording is saved verbatim in the foundational conversation. A small crop saving does not complete this requirement. The construction priority is propagation of anatomical information throughout the whole operation.

In *What an <Object> Is* §1.3, a squint removes variation irrelevant to the question while retaining exactly what its measurement says. Complementary views supply information the others leave out. In §1.5, the laws restrict admissible states, and known facts about related objects further constrain the local question. In §1.6, deriving consequences adds no independent evidence; it makes information already held computationally usable. These are the grounds of both cheaper work and stronger discrimination.

For the knee, a bone's resolved physical places constrain its contained marrow, adjacent cartilage, joint surfaces and attachments. Those constrain the tendons and muscles, which also constrain vessels and intervening fluid. Each useful resolution must sharpen dependent identity questions and their native slice/spatial domains before their expensive measurements run. Their new native evidence then refines the same common map and the remaining questions. A projected expectation remains a prior; the acquired local reading supplies evidence. Near misses, ties and unavailable information stay named.

For each family, inspect the concrete chain: which known anatomy narrows it; which symbolic consequence is derived once; which identities/places remain plausible; which residual actually needs another native measurement; and which later family consumes its result. Use existing plain functions, actual data and typed anatomical relations. Do not add a generic rule engine or another execution graph. The stages should increasingly ask small, anatomically constrained questions as more of the knee is resolved. Kernel acceleration and caching support this mechanism; they are not its completion criterion. Measure the whole operation: a smaller local reader may still be followed by another family's full-frame request, so local area savings alone do not establish whole-pipeline efficiency.

## Significance weighting: structural amplification in this build

Rohan explicitly supplied `/Users/rohanvinaik/Projects/rohan-vinaik.github.io/papers/Core Documents/SIGNIFICANCE_WEIGHTING.md`; the lead read the complete paper. The following is the lead's application of its mechanism to this recognition layer, under Rohan's anatomical specification.

Sections 4–5 describe correlated coverage: one useful fact can resolve a cluster of related questions, leaving a smaller independent residual. Section 16 orders construction by the cheapest step that can falsify the most. Here the value of a cheap anatomical reading includes every dependent identity and measurement domain it narrows. Known bone and joint geometry should constrain contained marrow, articular surfaces, intervening wedges and attachment courses together. The following measurements then answer these already constrained questions, rather than rediscovering each object independently over the whole acquisition.

The bridge example in §13 explains why complementary information can have more value together than separately. In this build, independently native planes of the same object jointly establish a physical reference; that reference can make several later searches usable at once. This is an application of complementarity, not a claim that the paper's rule-promotion theorem or numerical scaling bounds have been proved for MRI. Its measured constants belong to their original graphs. No corpus induction, significance scoring engine or later AI mechanism is added here.

Sections 14 and 16.4 supply two concrete engineering lessons. A derived expectation is useful scheduling information, not fresh confirmation of itself. Also, deciding which work can change must consider information already derived, not only original inputs: a native description obtained later in the run can newly constrain a dependent question. Trace that handoff through the actual common-map refresh and next consumer. Keep an honest unresolved result where the needed observation is unavailable; useful priors do not require perfect boundaries first.

The construction check for each family is therefore: **which cluster of alternatives did the existing structure remove, which native question remains, and which later questions does its answer narrow?** Record the actual producer and consumers beside the measurement's slice/window scope. Typed relation labels, smaller pixel counts, caching and faster kernels alone do not establish this propagation. Do not claim measured entropy or a guaranteed speed multiplier from these structural descriptions. The retention rule remains in force; the paper's consolidation discussion does not authorize dropping knee readings.

Rohan explicitly extended this to **every anatomical object**, with full reasoning from anatomical structure as an implementation of the `<object>` thesis. This applies retroactively to accepted descriptions as well as remaining views. For each emitted concept, trace its known neighbours and physical relation, the identity/location alternatives that relation constrains, the shared values already available, the residual complementary native measurements, and the consumers of its result. Containment, attachment, intervening space, course and physiological order must do actual work in that chain. Keep diffuse fields and local sections faithful to their meanings; a field does not need a fabricated unique point. Complete this through the existing R2–R6 families and their production consumers, retaining competent definitions and named uncertainty throughout.
