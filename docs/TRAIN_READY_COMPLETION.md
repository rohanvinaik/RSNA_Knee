## 1. The requested result and its boundaries

Rohan's requested result is a **hand-grounded, usable recognition corpus and an operational local
learning loop**, ready to train the designed Minsky/Winston Society of Mind. The 58 labelled studies
supply known clinical answers; actual pictures establish the anatomical presentations to which those
answers apply. Rough, partial but correctly named supports are useful teaching. Recognition tools
isolate those presentations; they need not solve every new image perfectly before the AI can learn.

The sequence is:

1. Inspect recognizable anatomical objects and normal/diagnosed presentations on their images.
2. Retain their measured bundles and scoped signed calls as individual K-line precedents.
3. Verify that ordinary recall and condition consumers use those meanings, including both normal
   and diagnosed configurations. Close actual teaching/readback gaps rather than optimize a score.
4. Use that corpus to gather more objects and experiences locally. Preserve corrections and sources.
5. Train contextual interpretations and useful procedures from those experiences; retain warranted
   significant differences and, when earned, censors/splits. Their memory must change what acts next.

Bucket construction enables this learning and remains part of runtime memory. It is not the entire
learning algorithm. A retained experience is not automatically a learned successful procedure.
Kaggle packaging, uploads, submission and a notebook performance campaign are outside this task.

## 2. Representation and authority contracts: do not substitute another objective

| Value | Meaning | Required handling |
|---|---|---|
| Signed call on a named question | −1 against, 0 unasserted/unknown, +1 for that question | Keep question, subject, support, views, source and reason. A negative identity is not a negative diagnosis or procedure judgment. |
| Named condition result | `assigned` = +1, `defeated` = −1, `open` = 0 on that condition question | Keep named reasons/conflicts as well as polarity. This is not a calibrated probability or permission to change the external readout. |
| A measured neutral HDC component | An observation at its declared reference | It is information. It is not a missing/unwritten bank. |
| Unread facet or unavailable measurement | No observation on that facet | Preserve its availability/silence explicitly; do not manufacture a neutral measurement. |
| Healthy reference | Zero departure on the particular defined measurement | A normal object's complete profile still has signed structure; it is not an all-zero vector. Do not fit a cohort percentile as the healthy reference. |
| Official CSV `0` / `1` | External known absence / presence | The label reader converts to −1 / +1. A blank remains no call. Convert explicitly only at an interface that actually expects CSV values. |
| Whole-condition precedent | Independently known condition state expressed by measured, identity-valid tissue | Warranted diagnosed **and whole-normal** configuration recall may decide, under the author's decisions. Context-only resemblance cannot acquire that authority. |
| Local/site finding | Evidence about its actual site | It may support or oppose an applicable claim; it does not silently establish every other site. |

Balanced ternary is the measurement/representation/reasoning substrate, not merely a three-way
output label. Keep exact named banks, directions, scale and availability through bundling and recall.
One object has one representation across its available views; full and cheap profiles coexist.
Do not fabricate full-resolution banks for a cheap-only case or collapse all views to one selected view.

Anatomy supplies conditional information, not just a final filter after blind search. Current-study
observations constrain subsequent questions. Femur and tibia can correctly resemble each other as
bones; their anatomical arrangement distinguishes their roles. Do not force their appearance apart,
make their names automatic countercases, or erase established anatomy with an appearance-only winner.
An expected window or ROLE_OBJECT tile is sampled support, not certification of its tissue. Missing
geometry is unknown; an available partial relation still carries information. Normal anatomical
expectations do not automatically exclude abnormal anatomy. Hard exclusions need an actual warranted
contradiction at the scope tested.

Correctly identified objects **not implicated by a labelled study's positive conditions are valid
normal examples**. No separate normality annotation is required. Positive multisite conditions retain
their actual scope: a fracture-positive study does not label every bone fractured. A known diagnosis
of one object carries to its other identity-confirmed views; rejected or unasserted identity views
cannot teach that diagnosis. Unlabelled objects remain identity-only.

## 4. Bounded implementation repairs

### R1 — repair the signed-label boundary and prove clinical teaching reaches the child

**Verified defect:** `squint_procedure_memory.read_label_table` returns known calls as −1/+1.
`prepare_squint_training._load_labels_and_corpus` returns that signed table. But
`_compose_round_teaching` accepts a complete row only when every value is in `(0, 1)`.
An ordinary complete row with any negative diagnosis is therefore silently discarded.
The reproduction uses the actual reader and a confirmed identity fixture: ACL is +1, the other
eleven questions are −1, yet no condition calls are carried and no configurations rebuilt.

**Producer → consumer:** CSV → `read_label_table` → `_load_labels_and_corpus` →
`_compose_round_teaching` → `carry_condition_calls` / configuration rebuilding → published memory →
ordinary object/configuration recall and condition combination.

Implementation:

1. Keep the reader's signed schema. Validate known ±1 calls and distinguish missing/unknown.
2. At existing APIs expecting CSV-style rows, translate known −1/+1 to 0/1 explicitly, or use their
   established typed adapter. Do not change every internal score to binary. Do not coerce an unknown
   with `value != 1` into absence. Preserve complete-row requirements where normality needs them;
   independently scoped known calls may still teach through existing routes.
3. Carry labels only through confirmed identity views and existing clinical scope rules. Reuse
   `carry_condition_calls`; do not write another implication table or normal-object classifier.
4. Preserve existing independently scoped teaching. A nonempty `condition_calls` map is not proof
   that every eligible question is present; inspect missing entries without overwriting a sourced
   conflict. A scoped contradiction remains evidence for review, not last-writer-wins.
5. Rebuild configurations for studies whose canonical teaching changed, including newly labelled
   identities. Regional question references retain their existing linked-reference publication route.
6. Export corpus/experience material from the **published child's** actual identity when representing
   post-round training material. Keep the pre-action material and parent identity explicit as well;
   do not label the parent's export as containing the child's newly added examples.

Two-sign intent tests:

- The actual CSV reader's normal and positive calls survive composition, publication, reload and
  ordinary condition consumption; an unimplicated object becomes normal under the authored rule.
- Blank/unknown, unlabelled study and unconfirmed view do not acquire a negative diagnosis.
- A study-level fracture presentation does not become a localized fracture on every bone.
- A newly eligible configuration appears; an unrelated existing signed case/configuration is retained.
- A partial existing clinical map gains only warranted missing calls; existing conflict is not erased.

### R2 — procedure recall must answer its own question, with an observed pre-action cue

**Code finding:** `_empirical_opposition_floor` now ignores differently named objects, but still
treats same-subject cases with an opposite clinical label, and same-subject identity-store cases,
as opposition to a procedure. Those are different questions. Healthy and diseased tissue can both
benefit from the same look; an identity failure can make a look useful rather than contraindicate it.
This is a verified source path and a semantic defect; the same-subject effect still needs its focused
runtime regression. Do not describe it as a measured failure rate.

`_row_cue_banks` also falls back to the first same-study/same-subject case when the row lacks its
observed cue. That case need not be the pre-action support. `_row_category` can label locate/profile
measurement completion `confirmed_resolution` without an independently resolved answer. Keep what
that completion actually establishes rather than relabelling it diagnostic success.

**Producer → consumer:** attributed request + pre-action observations + outcome → retained procedure
case → `memory_view` → `recall_procedure_experiences` → `select_observation_requests` /
`route_observation_request` → next actual action in `infer_study`.

Implementation:

1. Compare compatible named agency questions, action kinds, views/scales, supports and observed
   context. A procedure countercase must establish something against **that procedure's applicability
   or outcome here**. Do not derive it solely from diagnosis polarity or another anatomical name.
2. Retain relevant identity/clinical evidence as contextual observations with their actual meanings;
   only a recorded applicable reason can connect it to a procedural conclusion. Reuse existing
   signed-case/context machinery rather than create a new global vote or cosine threshold.
3. Recall against the actual observed pre-action cue. An explicitly linked equivalent saved support
   may supply it with provenance. An arbitrary first case or post-action profile may not. Missing or
   ambiguous cues remain retained but cannot claim contextually warranted procedural recall.
4. Preserve separate outcomes: new information, already available information, independently correct
   answer, independently contradicted answer, still open, action unavailable/not run. An action can
   be useful without settling the condition; successful execution alone is not diagnosis teaching.
5. A remembered unchanged/open answer must not globally veto the action. Historical resource failure
   applies only under the same relevant failure conditions; it must not suppress a currently available
   tool. Keep the existing bounded dispatch as fallback when memory has no applicable experience.
6. No strongest-bin equality gate: nearby HDC profiles remain nearby even if their dominant bin changes.
   Preserve the repaired continuous exact comparison; do not reintroduce a categorical proxy for it.

Two-sign intent tests:

- A useful checked experience changes ordering/selection in a compatible other study; a different
  question, context, action kind or incompatible/unobserved cue does not inherit it.
- Adding a normal case beside a diagnosed case does not itself veto a useful procedure; adding an
  applicable actual procedure failure can oppose it at the warranted scope.
- An identity negative is retained and usable for identity, without automatically becoming a procedure
  failure. Unknown remains unknown. Measured-neutral banks remain distinct from unread banks.
- Completion, wrong resolution, open new information and no reading retain distinct remedies.
- Excluding a source study removes its procedure experience and derived preparation as well as cases.

### R3 — preserve causal action attribution already being wired

**Existing improvement to retain:** `infer_study` now takes a condition/cue snapshot for each actual
request, acts, refreshes identity and the affected finding, and records that action's after-state.
`condition_state` reads direct attribution. Previously a later action's final answer could be assigned
to every earlier action on the question. Do not regress to batch-wide success labels.

Required checks:

- With two actions, the first leaves the question open and the second settles it: only the second
  receives the settled after-state. The second's before-state includes the first's observations.
- An action that exposes wrong identity can be informative; a state change against the independent
  answer is not success. Ambiguous old attribution remains ambiguous rather than reconstructed by guess.
- The condition consumer really reads refreshed identity; a changed identity in a report alone is not
  integration. Stop only work made unnecessary by established evidence, not compulsory evidence checks.

### R4 — close round pinning, safe resume and one-chain publication

**Remaining source findings:** `_code_identity` pins only three files although the round invokes the

publication but before `round-receipt.json`, an existing child is accepted by revision name without
checking that its contents are the child this round composed. Training-checkpoint reuse also needs
output/source validation, not just matching parent/label fields.

Implementation:

1. Pin the actual execution closure using the repository's existing source identity/bundle mechanism.
   Include the pipeline, agency consumers, memory/preparation and teaching/configuration producers;
   do not invent a second provenance framework. Changed behavior must invalidate incompatible resume.
2. Pin the ready study set, parent, labels/reports used, visual additions and policy inputs. Reject
   missing, malformed, changed or partially written inputs with named reasons; do not silently skip.
3. Validate checkpoint file digests and source identities. Record raw-input identities using the
   existing study/index receipts; a changing partially extracted study must not masquerade as frozen.
4. On partial publication, verify expected parent/lineage and the exact composed content or existing
   publication receipt before accepting a child. A reused revision name is insufficient. Never delete,
   mutate or silently replace a published child; refuse a mismatch for explicit recovery.
5. Resume after interruptions during study work, material export, child publication and final receipt.
   Completed valid work is reused; a fresh run and resumed run produce the same semantic child.
6. Preserve all parent signed cases, alternatives, sources and backing payloads. Rebuilt configurations
   track corrections without allowing superseded evidence to keep speaking through another derived case.

Two-sign intent tests: valid identical resume reuses work; changed code/input/labels/additions/parent,
tampered checkpoint and wrong same-revision child refuse. Interrupt after publishing the child and
before the final receipt: safe reuse succeeds only for the expected content. Null, empty, malformed,
duplicate and boundary inputs get their actual named outcomes. Test the real CLI as well as helpers.
